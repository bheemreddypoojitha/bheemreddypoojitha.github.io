Lab

Two imaging papers, running in your browser.

Both papers are about seeing what ordinary images hide. These demos are small, simplified versions you can poke at.

Under review · 2026

Nano crack detection using residual imaging

A nano-scale crack framework built on Edge Residual Imaging: advanced preprocessing, feature extraction and CNN-based segmentation for precise structural defect analysis.

The demo generates a noisy synthetic micrograph with a hidden crack, then walks it through a classical pipeline: smooth, subtract, threshold, clean up. Because the crack is known, the page scores itself.

  • Submitted to International Conference on Computational Intelligence in Data Science
  • Authors Bheemreddy Poojitha, R. Aruna Flarence, Mentapally Vaishnavi, Sudini Sanjana Reddy, Tumma Anusha

Illustration only. The paper's method adds CNN-based segmentation; this uses a classical filter chain on synthetic data.

Under review · 2025

Fake certificate detection with multispectral imaging and AI

An AI-driven forgery detector that images documents in UV and IR to verify holograms, analyze microtext and extract spectral features. Designed for real-time authentication on web and mobile.

The specimen on the left reacts to the page's spectral switch. Change the light and the hidden security layers appear.

  • Submitted to AIP Conference Proceedings
  • Authors R. Aruna Flarence, Bheemreddy Poojitha, Tumma Anusha, Mentapally Vaishnavi, Sudini Sanjana Reddy
  • Stack CNN, Siamese networks, Fourier hologram analysis, OCR microtext, Flask
